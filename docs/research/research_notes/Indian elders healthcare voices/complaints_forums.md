# Indian elders' healthcare voices: consumer-complaint sites and discussion forums

## 1. Header

**Scope.** Real, dated, verbatim complaints from adults aged 60+ in India, pensioners, or the family members who help them, about hospitals, pharmacies, health apps, health insurance, CGHS (and the related ECHS and railway schemes) and Ayushman Bharat / PM-JAY. This file covers problems and evidence only. It makes no product or feature suggestions and includes no tremor or pointer topics.

**Sites searched and used (5 sites, 62 items):**
- consumercomplaints.in: 37 items.
- staffnews.in reader comments: 22 items. This is a central-government pensioner news site; comments were read through its public WordPress comments endpoint.
- team-bhp.com forum: 8 items.
- mouthshut.com: 3 items.
- voxya.com: 1 item.

**Sites tried with no usable result:** complaintboard.in, gconnect.in, centralgovernmentnews.com, govtempdiary, 90paisa, LocalCircles, Reddit. Section 4 gives the reasons.

**Conventions:**
- Each “…” in the quote column is a separate verbatim excerpt, and **+** separates distinct excerpts from the same post; nothing is stitched together. A leading or trailing "..." marks an omission within a sentence.
- Original spelling, grammar, capitals and Hinglish/Telugu are kept. Line breaks and runs of spaces were collapsed to single spaces.
- Names of complainants, patients, doctors and staff, plus phone, policy, claim and beneficiary numbers and addresses, were removed using "...".
- "Seen via = page" means the full page (raw HTML/JSON) was downloaded and the quote was copied from it. No item rests on a search snippet alone.
- Where a family complaint does not state the parent's age, the Pain field says "age not stated". These are probably, but not certainly, 60+.

**Tag definitions:**
- Speaker: who wrote the post.
- Did-task: who actually performed the healthcare task described.
- Severity flags:
  - missed-care: care delayed, denied or disrupted.
  - wrong-medicine: wrong or substituted medicine.
  - lost-money: out-of-pocket loss, unpaid refund or claim.
  - forced-dependence: the elder could not manage without someone else.

- CMP-23: CGHS local advisory committee meetings described as useless.
- A related Team-BHP post (#6066210, 7 Dec 2025, not about a parent) found NCH and IRDAI complaints are "forwarded to the insurance company".

**Porting or switching insurer** is used, but blocked by pre-existing disease or by the insurer.
- CMP-40: the agent mocked porting, but the child ported anyway.
- CMP-45: a diabetic father is refused by Niva Bupa and Manipal Cigna and offered only "Care Senior (which really seems to be a basic plan with a lot of exclusions)". The child is now considering Arogya Sanjeevani. The family also brought the father's HbA1c down through diet, without success in getting cover.
- CMP-41: the child tried to drop the mother from the floater to save premium and got no response.

- CMP-46: this failed. "we check only page 1 and page 3 of the policy and nothing else".
- CMP-36: the hospital desk itself caused the rejection, and the advice given afterwards was to "coordinate with the hospital insurance desk".

**Abandoning cashless and paying cash**:
- CMP-51: the family chose the government tariff over cashless.
- CMP-36: paid in full and is pursuing reimbursement.
- CMP-35: forced to pay out of pocket.

**Going to a cheaper hospital elsewhere**:
- CMP-52: "Then I went to Vijayawada for my mothers treatment, there we paid just 35, 000/-".

**Switching from the app to a physical pharmacy**:
- CMP-09: "I would prefer to go to a offline pharmacy". The reason is the app's new oncology-prescription rule.

**Rescheduling deliveries, AI bot and human escalation**:
- CMP-07: the cycle repeated daily. The human ticket was promised a 24–48 hour resolution and "No one contacted me".

**Family member checks medicines at home**:
- CMP-11: a daughter caught the wrong-strength BP tablets because the mother "doesn’t know how to read". This shows forced dependence.

**Fresh CGHS referral**:

**Repeated in-person office visits by the elder**:

- CMP-18: "could not meet despite many efforts".

**Asking on pensioner news-site comment threads** for rules on reimbursement and items:

**Not naming the offender for fear of reprisal**:

**NGO or hospital staff enrolling patients**:
