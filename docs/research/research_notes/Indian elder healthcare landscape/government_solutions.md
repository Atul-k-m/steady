# Indian government digital and service solutions for older adults' (60+) healthcare: landscape, adoption, and documented gaps

Scope note: India only. "Today" = 2026-11-15. Every number below carries the date the source gives. "Secondary" means a news, aggregator or exam-prep site reporting an official figure; I could not open the primary PIB or Parliament document in those cases (eparlib.sansad.in refused connections during this session). Journey-stage tags: S1 Find doctor, S2 Book, S3 Register, S4 Wait/navigate, S5 Consultation, S6 Tests, S7 Reports, S8 Medicines, S9 Follow-ups, S10 Payments/insurance, S11 Digital health IDs/teleconsult, S12 Emergencies, S0 Other. The stage tags are my classification of what each service does. No source assigned them.

## Q1. What adoption numbers exist (official dashboards, PIB, Parliament answers), and with what date?

### Takeaway
Headline adoption numbers are large and recent for the central digital rails: about 50 crore eSanjeevani consultations (Sept 2026), 93 crore+ ABHA accounts (July 2026), 23–25 crore Scan & Share/Scan & Register OPD tokens (June–Aug 2026), about 1.14 crore Vay Vandana cards (Feb 2026, secondary), 29 lakh+ Elderline calls (Sept 2026, secondary), 47.75 lakh Tele-MANAS calls (Oct 2026) and 20,547 Jan Aushadhi Kendras (July 2026). Several of the services older people rely on most have no current official count: NIC ORS (latest is 2022), NPHCE district geriatric units (latest found is 2016), and the CGHS pensioner share.

#### eSanjeevani (rows 1–2)
- eSanjeevani passed 50 crore cumulative teleconsultations across all States/UTs. The service averages 2.5–3 lakh consultations per day, with a single-day peak above 6 lakh. It has 1.42 lakh+ Ayushman Arogya Mandir spokes, 19,000+ hubs, 901 online OPDs, 2 lakh+ registered providers and 15 languages. Patient-to-doctor specialist OPDs are live at AIIMS Jodhpur, AIIMS Jammu and AIIMS Patna. C-DAC Mohali has provided the technology since inception, under MoHFW guidance. — [AIR News (newsonair.gov.in), 6 Oct 2026](https://newsonair.gov.in/esanjeevani-achieves-milestone-of-50-cr-teleconsultations-from-across-the-country/)
- Exact count 50,00,92,730; the 50-crore mark was crossed on 15 Sep 2026; women 57.14% of use; Gold Award, National Awards for e-Governance 2026. — [DD News, 6 Oct 2026 (credited to PIB)](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/esanjeevani-crosses-50-crore-milestone-women-account-for-57-of-utilisation/); [Chronicle India](https://www.chronicleindia.in/current-affairs/18227-e-sanjeevani-crosses-50-crore-teleconsultations-mark) (accessed 2026-11-15)
- Earlier: 13,91,61,458 total teleconsultations as of 19 Jul 2023, and 2,08,569 spokes connected as of 14 Jul 2023 (Lok Sabha written reply, MoS Bharati Pravin Pawar). — [Indian Pharma Post, 24 Jul 2023](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/policy/1391-crore-teleconsultations-have-been-completedpib-14421)
- Split between AB-HWC (provider-to-provider) and eSanjeevani OPD (patient-to-doctor): NOT FOUND in the 2026 releases I read. Neither the AIR nor the 2023 Lok Sabha report gives it.

#### ABDM / ABHA / Scan & Share (rows 3–4)
- 104 crore+ health records are linked to 93 crore+ ABHA accounts. The May 2026 milestone was 100 crore records and 90 crore ABHAs. ABDM launched in Sept 2021. ABHA is a 14-digit ID. — [PIB backgrounder "Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission: India's Digital Health Backbone", 6 Jul 2026](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jul/doc202676912801.pdf)
- Scan and Share (NHA, launched 2022): patients scan a QR at the facility to get a digital queue token, and their demographic details are pulled from ABHA into the facility HIS. An IIHMR study found waits fell "from approximately one hour to just 2 to 5 minutes". Over 23.21 crore ABHA-linked tokens had been issued as on 18 Jun 2026. — [same PIB backgrounder, 6 Jul 2026](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jul/doc202676912801.pdf)
- Renamed "Scan and Register": 25 crore OPD registrations since Oct 2022; about 4 lakh per day; 30,800 facilities (24,323 public, 6,481 private) in 756 districts across 36 States/UTs. Top facilities: AIIMS New Delhi 54.29 lakh, AIIMS Bhopal 29.10 lakh, AIIMS Bhubaneswar 23.93 lakh. Top states: Bihar 7.30 crore, UP 4.09 crore, AP 3.38 crore. — [Digital Health News, "last updated 10 Aug 2026" (secondary; original date unclear)](https://www.digitalhealthnews.com/abha-based-scan-and-register-service-crosses-25-cr-opd-registrations-nha)
- Earlier milestones: 365 hospitals (Feb 2023); 3 crore registrations / 5,435 facilities (Jun 2024); 4 crore / 8,270 facilities; 17,000+ facilities and 6.6 crore registrations (PIB, Dec 2024, via search summary). — [PIB, Feb 2023](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1901721); [ThePrint, Jun 2024](https://theprint.in/india/abhas-scan-and-share-service-facilitates-3-crore-opd-registrations-nationwide/2120140); [MoHFW press 7476](https://mohfw.gov.in/press-info/7476)
- Aarogya Setu 2.0 was launched on 29 Jun 2026 as the citizen app for ABHA creation, records, "Scan & Register", booking of teleconsultations and in-person visits via UHI, and a PM-JAY wallet view via NHCX. UHI has five live services, including ambulance booking and doctor consultation. Digital Health Incentive Scheme payouts as on 18 Jun 2026: hospitals Rs 107+ crore; diagnostics/labs/pharmacies Rs 2.95 crore; digital solution companies Rs 26+ crore. — [PIB backgrounder, 6 Jul 2026](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jul/doc202676912801.pdf)

#### NIC ORS / e-Hospital / e-Sushrut (rows 5, 15)
- 700+ hospitals in 29 States/UTs onboarded on e-Hospital; ORS 54.5 lakh+ appointments booked. — [NIC Informatics, April 2022](https://informatics.nic.in/files/informatics-flipbook-Apr-2022/files/basic-html/page10.html). An undated article gives 475+ hospitals and 58.40 lakh+ appointments, which conflicts with this. — [Kashmir Vision (accessed 2026-11-15)](https://kashmirvision.in/?p=161202)
- How ORS works: it is a Digital India framework linking government hospitals for online OPD appointments, integrated with ABHA. The common patient portal ors.gov.in also offers report viewing and blood-availability status. Booking is by Aadhaar number, choice of hospital/department and date, then an SMS confirmation. A mobile number linked to Aadhaar is required. — [PIB explainer, Jan 2024 (via search summary)](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2024/jan/doc2024115298601.pdf); [Vikaspedia e-health portal (accessed 2026-11-15)](https://vikaspedia.in/e-governance/digital-india/e-health-portal)
- AIIMS Delhi appointment channels: web, call centre, IVRS and on-site counters. Web portal started 29 Sep 2014. — [AIIMS appointment system note (undated)](https://aiims.edu/images/pdf/notice/Digital%20AIIMS/appointment%20system-white.pdf)
- Historic baseline: 1.49 lakh AIIMS patients had used ORS by 22 Feb 2016 (Lok Sabha). — [Business Standard/PTI, Feb 2016](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/about-1-5-lakh-aiims-patients-benfited-from-ors-govt-116022600379_1.html)
- Current (2025–26) ORS hospital and appointment counts: NOT FOUND.
- eSushrut@Clinic (C-DAC), launched June 2026: a plug-and-play HMIS for small clinics, gated through HPR/HFR verification; 2,200+ facilities onboarded. — [PIB backgrounder, 6 Jul 2026](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jul/doc202676912801.pdf)

#### Elderline 14567 (row 6)
- Launched as the country's first pan-India helpline for senior citizens (operational from 1 Oct 2021). It grew out of a 2017 Tata Trusts initiative with the Telangana government through its partner Vijayavahini Charitable Foundation. Tata Trusts and NSE Foundation were technical partners to MoSJE. — [PIB (MoSJE), PRID 1758880, 28 Sep 2021](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1758880)
- Fully operational in 23 States/UTs with 790+ professionals taking calls (FY2021-22). — [Tata Trusts Annual Report 2021-22, p.16](https://www.tatatrusts.org/ebook/tata-trusts-annual-report-2021-22/files/basic-html/page16.html)
- Over 29 lakh calls since launch; 8.67 lakh+ actionable calls (information, guidance, emotional support, field intervention) since FY2021, up to 21 Sep 2026; operational in 32 States/UTs. NISD monitors implementation under MoSJE's Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY). State Connect Centres coordinate field interventions. — [SuperKalam current affairs citing PIB, 24 Sep 2026 (secondary; PIB original not located)](https://superkalam.com/current-affairs/24-09-2026/elderline-14567-provides-information-guidance-and-support-to-senior-citizens-across-india-launched-on-1st-october-2021-1d4ac0e4-602a-468e-95e6-e77421bbc43f)

- Kerala: 1.5 lakh+ seniors helped since launch (to 20 Nov 2025), about 112 calls/day, 300+ calls needed on-ground intervention. — [Onmanorama, 29 Nov 2025](https://www.onmanorama.com/lifestyle/health/2025/11/29/elder-line-helpline-senior-citizens-kerala.html). ## Q3. What documented barriers exist for older users of each (studies, audits, committee reports, news)?

### Takeaway
Documented elder barriers cluster around identity and phone dependence: Aadhaar–mobile linkage for OTP, fingerprint e-KYC failure, OTP going only to the primary cardholder (CGHS). ## Q4. Who funds and operates each?

### Takeaway
Central MoHFW and its agencies fund and run most digital rails: NHA (ABDM, PM-JAY/Vay Vandana), C-DAC (eSanjeevani, eSushrut) and Dte. CGHS. NIC/MeitY runs ORS/e-Hospital. MoSJE runs Elderline through NISD with philanthropic technical partners. Department of Pharmaceuticals/PMBI runs Jan Aushadhi. State governments fund their own programmes (TN MTM, Kerala eHealth and Vayomithram, Karnataka ABArK). Vay Vandana depends on centre-state premium sharing, which has stalled rollout in some states.

### Cited Findings
- eSanjeevani: built by C-DAC; C-DAC Mohali has provided end-to-end technology development since inception; MoHFW "guides its evolution". The article does not state a funding line. — [AIR News, 6 Oct 2026](https://newsonair.gov.in/esanjeevani-achieves-milestone-of-50-cr-teleconsultations-from-across-the-country/)
- ABDM / Scan & Share / UHI: run by the National Health Authority; the UHI gateway is operated by NHA. ### Inferences
- The centre-state split creates uneven elder coverage. Vay Vandana is centrally designed but depends on state premium sharing and state-scheme integration (Kerala not implementing as of Nov 2025; Karnataka routing via ABArK). An elderly person's actual access depends on their state, not only on holding the card.
- Elderline's operating model has moved from philanthropy-backed state centres (2021–22) to a NISD-monitored zonal model (2026). Who runs the zonal hubs is not documented in my sources.
